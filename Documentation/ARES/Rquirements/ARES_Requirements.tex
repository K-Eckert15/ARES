% =========================================================
% FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
% =========================================================

\begin{enumerate}

    \item The vehicle shall provide secure
    mechanical mounting and sufficient
    internal space for all flight-critical
    electronics and experimental payloads.

    \item The vehicle shall incorporate
    an active roll-stabilization system
    capable of regulating vehicle
    rotation during its intended
    operational flight envelope.

    \item The vehicle shall maintain
    an acceptable stability margin
    throughout its approved
    flight configuration.

    \item The vehicle shall support
    compatible propulsion hardware
    and provide positive motor
    retention throughout flight.

    \item The vehicle shall incorporate
    a dual-deployment recovery system
    consisting of drogue and
    main parachute recovery stages.

    \item The recovery system shall
    be designed to return the vehicle
    and experimental payloads
    within an established
    landing velocity limit.

    \item The vehicle shall permit
    the removal and replacement
    of experimental payloads
    without requiring structural
    modification of the airframe.

    \item The completed vehicle shall
    undergo structural inspection,
    functional verification,
    and integrated ground testing
    prior to flight.

    \item The vehicle shall operate
    within the restrictions
    established by the approved
    launch location and
    applicable launch procedures.

\end{enumerate}
