% =========================================================
% FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
% =========================================================

\begin{enumerate}

    \item The system shall acquire onboard inertial
    and position measurements to characterize
    the vehicle's motion, orientation, and trajectory.

    \item The system shall transmit flight
    measurements and system status to the
    receiving ground station during flight.

    \item The system shall record flight
    measurements in onboard nonvolatile
    memory independently of the RF link.

    \item The system shall preserve recorded
    data during temporary communication
    interruptions.

    \item The system shall provide telemetry
    containing timestamps, sensor measurements,
    position information, and system status.

    \item The ground station shall receive,
    decode, and display flight telemetry
    in real time.

    \item The ground station shall support
    recording and exporting received
    telemetry for postflight analysis.

    \item The system shall support preflight
    verification of sensor initialization,
    GPS operation, power status,
    and RF communication.

    \item The system shall provide a method
    for retrieving recorded flight data
    following vehicle recovery.

    \item The system shall maintain
    communication compatibility with
    the selected ground-station receiver.

    \item The system shall operate
    independently of flight-critical
    recovery electronics and experimental
    communications payloads.

    \item The subsystem shall be securely
    mounted within the nose cone and
    maintain its mechanical and electrical
    connections during flight.

\end{enumerate}
