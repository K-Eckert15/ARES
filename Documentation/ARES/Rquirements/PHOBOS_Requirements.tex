    \item The system shall be securely mounted
    within the rocket to ensure safety
    during flight.

    \item The system shall feature active beam
    steering utilizing onboard flight data
    to direct the transmitted signal toward
    the receiving ground station.

    \item The system shall provide experimental
    RF measurements suitable for evaluating
    beam-steering performance.

    \item The system shall transmit decodable
    data to the receiving ground station.

    \item The system shall use locking
    connectors for signal and power
    connections between circuit boards
    to maintain electrical connectivity
    during flight.

    \item The system shall electronically
    control the relative phase of its
    antenna elements to steer the
    transmitted beam.
